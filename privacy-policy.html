Last updated: October 16, 2026

Depending on the features you use, the app may process account and sign-in information, information you submit, virtual trading activity, watchlists, holdings, and app or technical information needed to operate and protect the service.

Information may be used to provide sign-in, maintain your app session, operate virtual trading features, save app activity, support multiplayer features, troubleshoot issues, and protect the service.

TradeVeda provides simulated trading for learning and practice. Virtual balances and simulated transactions are not real money, and the app does not represent virtual trading results as guaranteed financial returns.

Information may be processed by service providers used to operate the app, such as authentication, hosting, or backend infrastructure providers. We do not claim that no information is shared; actual sharing depends on the services and features enabled in the published app.

Information is retained only as needed for the purposes described in this policy or as required by applicable law. For questions or deletion requests, contact us using the details below. Requests may be subject to verification and legal or operational requirements.

7. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information, but no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

TradeVeda is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so the matter can be reviewed.
